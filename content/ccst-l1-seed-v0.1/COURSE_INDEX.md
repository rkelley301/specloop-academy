## Release gate

Do not publish, sell, assign to learners as approved technical training, or use to direct field work until an experienced I&C professional reviews and signs off on every lesson.

## Reviewer checklist

For each lesson, verify:

- Technical statements and formulas against authoritative, current references.
- Correct distinctions, assumptions, units, and limits for the topic.
- Each question and answer key; confirm scenario answers follow from the stated facts.
- Safety language against the intended audience and applicable site practices.
- Any `[VERIFY]` item and all model-specific details, if added later.
- No copyrighted standards text, paid study-guide text, proprietary exam questions, or confidential procedures have been copied into the lesson.
- Narration reads clearly aloud, including acronyms, units, symbols, and pauses.

Record reviewer, date, references checked, findings, and approval status outside the lesson or in the product's review metadata. A review label should only change to approved after the responsible reviewer signs off.
