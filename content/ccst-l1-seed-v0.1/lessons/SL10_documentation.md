# SL10 — Close the work order with useful records
> **DRAFT — TECHNICAL REVIEW REQUIRED.** This lesson is original educational draft content and has not been technically validated. Do not publish, sell, or use it as approved training until a qualified instrumentation and controls reviewer verifies the technical details, answer key, and safety language.

**Objective:** Identify the details that make a maintenance record useful for traceability and future troubleshooting.

## Audio script

A maintenance record should let the next person understand what was reported, what was observed, what work was done, and what condition remained afterward. A short entry can be useful if it is specific.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

Record the equipment or loop identification, the original symptom, relevant operating conditions, checks performed, measured values when applicable, parts or settings changed, the result, and any follow-up needed. Include dates and required references to calibration equipment or procedures when the work requires them.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

Separate fact from interpretation. “Input remained at 12.0 mA during the approved test” is an observation. “Transmitter is bad” is a conclusion and should be supported by evidence. If the cause remains uncertain, say so and record the next action.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

Good records protect continuity. They help detect repeat failures, show the condition before and after work, and make handoff clearer. Follow your site’s required forms, naming conventions, approvals, and record-retention rules.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

Before closing, ask: can another technician understand what happened without asking me to reconstruct it from memory? If not, add the missing observation or follow-up detail.

## Recall check
1. Name four details that make a work record useful.
2. How should an uncertain cause be recorded?
3. Why separate observations from conclusions?

## Scenario
A work note says only “fixed level transmitter.” What information would you add so the next technician can understand the fault and the work performed?

## Answer key
1. Examples: equipment/tag, symptom, conditions, checks, values, work performed, settings/parts changed, result, follow-up, references.
2. State that the cause is unconfirmed and document evidence plus next action.
3. It prevents an unsupported guess from being mistaken for a measured fact.

**Scenario answer:** Add the tag, reported symptom, relevant readings and conditions, checks performed, confirmed cause if known, repair or adjustment, final result, and any remaining action, using site-required documentation.
