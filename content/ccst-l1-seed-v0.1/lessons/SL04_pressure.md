# SL04 — Pressure measurement: gauge, absolute, and differential

**Objective:** Explain the reference used by gauge, absolute, and differential pressure measurements.

## Audio script

A pressure number is meaningful only when you know what it is compared against. Gauge pressure compares a pressure to local atmospheric pressure. A tire gauge is a familiar example: it reports pressure above the surrounding air.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

Absolute pressure compares pressure to a vacuum reference. At the surrounding atmosphere, absolute pressure is greater than zero. Gauge pressure and absolute pressure therefore use different reference points.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

Differential pressure, often shortened to DP, compares pressure at one connection with pressure at another. The result is the difference between the two sides. A DP transmitter can measure a pressure drop across equipment, or it can be arranged to infer level in a vessel. The installation and configuration determine what that difference means.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

When a pressure reading seems wrong, ask: What are the two pressure points? What reference is being used? Are the connections and impulse lines arranged as shown on the drawing? Is the instrument ranged and scaled for the intended measurement?

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

Do not open, vent, or disconnect process connections as a casual troubleshooting step. Pressure systems can contain hazardous energy or process material. Follow the site isolation and work authorization process.

## Recall check
1. What does gauge pressure use as its reference?
2. What does absolute pressure use as its reference?
3. What does a DP transmitter compare?

## Scenario
A DP transmitter is connected across a filter. The operator reports that the reading increased. What does that trend generally suggest, and what should be confirmed before concluding the filter is plugged?

## Answer key
1. Local atmospheric pressure.
2. A vacuum reference.
3. Pressure at its high-side connection versus pressure at its low-side connection.

**Scenario answer:** It suggests the pressure difference across the filter increased. Confirm the transmitter connections, operating conditions, signal integrity, and applicable maintenance criteria before diagnosing blockage.
