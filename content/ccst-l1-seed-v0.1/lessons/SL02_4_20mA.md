# SL02 — What a 4–20 mA signal tells you

**Objective:** Explain the meaning of live zero and calculate a linear signal value.

## Audio script

In a common analog instrument loop, four milliamps represents the bottom of the configured measurement range and twenty milliamps represents the top. The signal is often called live zero because a valid low-end measurement is above zero milliamps.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

That offset can help distinguish a valid low reading from some faults, but it does not diagnose a fault by itself. A broken wire, a device fault, or a system-specific alarm may produce different behavior. Always check the instrument and input-channel documentation.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

For a linear range, convert the measured current to a fraction of span. Subtract four milliamps, then divide by sixteen milliamps. That gives the fraction from zero to one. Multiply by the configured engineering-unit span and add the lower range value.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

Example: a zero-to-one-hundred psi range at twelve milliamps. Twelve minus four is eight. Eight divided by sixteen is one-half. Half of the range is fifty psi.

[PAUSE 3 SECONDS]

The relationship is: process value equals lower range value plus current minus four, divided by sixteen, times span. Confirm the configured range and signal type before using the result. A device may be configured for square-root extraction or another transfer function, so do not assume every loop is linear.

## Recall check
1. On a linear 4–20 mA loop, what fraction of span is 12 mA?
2. What does “live zero” mean here?
3. Name one reason the simple linear calculation may not apply.

## Scenario
A transmitter is configured from 0 to 200 degrees. The loop current is 8 mA and the configured transfer is linear. What value should the signal represent?

## Answer key
1. 50% of span: (12−4)/16 = 0.5.
2. The low end of the measurement range is represented by 4 mA, rather than 0 mA.
3. The loop may use a non-linear transfer function, such as square-root extraction, or have different configured endpoints.

**Scenario answer:** 50 degrees. Eight milliamps is 25% of span; 25% of 200 is 50.
